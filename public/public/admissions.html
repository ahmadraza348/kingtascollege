Admission process

Six steps from interest to enrollment

  • 1

    Choose a program

    Review nursing and allied health programs and select the one that matches your interests and qualifications.

  • 2

    Check eligibility

    Confirm the entry requirements for your chosen program with the admissions office.

  • 3

    Prepare documents

    Collect educational certificates, CNIC/B-Form, photographs and any additional documents advised.

  • 4

    Submit application

    Complete the online application form or submit your application at the campus admissions office.

  • 5

    Admissions review

    The admissions team reviews applications and contacts applicants regarding next steps.

  • 6

    Enrollment

    Complete enrollment formalities and join the session as advised by the college.

Required documents

  • Educational certificates and mark sheets
  • CNIC or B-Form (applicant)
  • CNIC copy (father/guardian)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Domicile, where applicable
  • Character certificate, where applicable
  • Any other documents advised by the office
Please confirm final requirements with the Admissions Office.
